The pilot year, in the open.

Our material is new and untested at scale, so we are building in public. Each gate has a written threshold, and nothing is sold until a food-contact attorney confirms the legal pathway.

Questions roasters ask.

Is DeCafe processing coffee today?

No. DeCafe is a proposed business. The plan starts with a pilot year on a single small line, and paid processing begins only after the science gates pass and the legal pathway is confirmed.

Is it really solvent-free?

No chlorinated solvent touches your coffee. The bean loop uses only water and green coffee extract. Food-grade ethanol is used offline to empty the columns, never on beans, and every regenerated column is rinsed and checked for residual solvent before it returns to service.

How do you keep my origin’s flavor separate?

We process one origin group per run with its own dedicated extract, and clean the vessel and change the extract between runs. Mesh baskets keep beans apart, but the liquid is what matters, so we do not share it. Blind cupping decides whether any lots may share a run.

Do I still own my coffee?

Yes. Toll processing means you send your own green coffee, pay a fee per pound, and get the same coffee back decaffeinated.

What happens to the caffeine?

It is recovered, purified and sold as a natural ingredient. That second product is what lets small lots be priced fairly. If the caffeine line were not viable, the toll service is designed to stand on its own.

Is the process FDA-approved?

Not yet. The gel’s ingredients are food ingredients, but the assembled gel needs its own clearance, and a food-contact attorney will determine the correct pathway. Nothing is sold until that authorization is in effect.

Why is the minimum only 250 pounds?

Our line is sized for origin lots, not containers. A setup fee covers the fixed work on each lot, so small lots pay their own way instead of being subsidized or refused.

Have a lot that is too small for everyone else?

Tell us about it. Pilot roasters cup their own coffee, decaffeinated, beside their control.
